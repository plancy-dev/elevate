This sample post is **fully public**.

Anyone can read the full content without signing in and without an active subscription.

## What to verify

- Logged-out users can open and read everything.
- Logged-in free users can open and read everything.
- Paid users can also read everything.

## Why this exists

This is a QA fixture so we can quickly confirm that `access_tier: public` bypasses all gates.
