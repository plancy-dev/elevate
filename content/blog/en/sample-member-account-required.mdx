This sample post is **member-only**.

If you are signed in, you should see the full article.
If you are logged out, you should see a preview plus the member CTA.

## Verification checklist

1. Open while logged out: preview + "Create free account" CTA should appear.
2. Sign in with a free account: full content should become visible.
3. Paid account should also read full content (as an authenticated member).

## Additional section for preview cutoff

We intentionally added extra paragraphs so the preview cutoff is easy to spot during QA.
This helps confirm that the member gate and premium gate have different CTA messaging.
