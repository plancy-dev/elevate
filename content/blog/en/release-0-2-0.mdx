Short-form teams juggle prompts, settings, and links across a bunch of tools. **Studio Productions** is where Elevate keeps that work in one **episode**—so nothing gets lost between Runway, chat apps, and your channel.

v0.2.0 makes that ledger easier to **read** and **maintain**:

## What’s new

- **Clearer labels** — When you add an artifact, you get friendly suggestions for roles (like script, prompt, or settings) so the list stays scannable for your team.
- **Order you can see** — Each step shows its place in the story, so everyone agrees what comes first in the cut.
- **Reorder without friction** — Change step order when the plan shifts—right from the episode page.
- **Help for Runway Shorts** — If you produce with Runway, the episode screen adds a short tip that points you to the same step-by-step guides your operators already use—next to the app, not buried in a repo.

Smoother validation when something can’t be saved, in every language we support.
