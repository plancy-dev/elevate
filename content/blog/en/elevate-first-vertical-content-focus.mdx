Earlier this year we kept asking “what ships next.” Today the public surface is still mostly **waitlist + auth**, plus the **content and automation backbone** we already run. Shipping more app features before the story earns attention felt backwards. We’d rather **publish writing worth reading** — consistent with the **media-first** decision (ADR-012, in-repo).

**Our first vertical is sajangnote** — a **marketing diagnosis** assistant for **solo café, dessert, and bakery** owners: paste shop context, get a score and an action plan. It is **not an Elevate feature**. New domain, new repo, new brand, **no code imported from here**. Pricing, spec, and demos belong on **sajangnote’s own channels**; this post is only the **direction share** for the Elevate audience.

**Why that wedge:** several “desire-axis” consumer verticals in Korea already have dominant players who have moved **fast on AI**; pitching there means burning budget on **headwind**. The gap we’re testing first is closer to **SMB / self-employed AI assistance**, narrowed to **single-owner hospitality retail**. Deeper market notes will live in **sajangnote `docs/decisions/`**.

**What Elevate does next (~3 months):** pause **new product UI/features, new marketing pages, and cosmetic redesigns** unless safety demands it. We **ship blog posts 3×/week** (Mon/Wed/Fri), **newsletter 1×/week** (Thursday), grow **SEO + email**, and keep **INIT/STAB** work (incidents, deps, PostHog). **No sajagnote implementation in this repo.**

You’ll see **builder-facing insights** here — workflows, prompts, SaaS ops, founder notes — rotating topics so this doesn’t turn into a small-business-only feed (that ICP is **sajangnote**).

**Takeaway:** Follow along via the blog and newsletter. **[Join the waitlist →](/#waitlist)** if you want the quiet ping. Launch details for sajagnote will come **separately** when that product is ready to speak for itself.
